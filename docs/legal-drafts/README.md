# FYNX legal and store-compliance drafts

These files are preparation material only. They are **not published legal notices, not user-accepted terms, and not proof of Paystack or Google Play approval**.

- `FYNX_PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md` — source-grounded privacy disclosure draft.
- `FYNX_TERMS_AND_COMMUNITY_STANDARDS_DRAFT.md` — user terms and UGC/community standards.
- `FYNX_MARKETPLACE_SHIPPING_RETURNS_REFUNDS_DRAFT.md` — marketplace fulfilment, returns, refunds and disputes.

Do not publish any file until:
1. The legal operator, address and monitored contacts are confirmed.
2. Production providers, regions, data flows, retention and deletion behavior are verified.
3. The account-deletion workflow and public request resource are implemented and tested.
4. Seller KYC, merchant-of-record, payment flow, funds custody, payout and wallet behavior are confirmed with Paystack.
5. The human moderation process and versioned terms acceptance are operational.
6. A qualified reviewer checks the documents for the launch jurisdictions.

The source-backed technical inventory and release checklist remain in `docs/PRIVACY_DATA_INVENTORY.md`, `docs/ACCOUNT_DELETION_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/GOOGLE_PLAY_READINESS.md`. Those source documents are not substitutes for final legal policies.
