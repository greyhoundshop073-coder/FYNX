This policy must be reviewed against Nigerian consumer law and every launch market. It must also match the actual checkout, order, seller fulfilment, dispute and payment-provider behavior before being published or supplied to Paystack.

## 1. Marketplace roles

FYNX provides a platform for buyers to discover products and interact with independent sellers. Unless an order expressly identifies FYNX or another party as the fulfilment provider, the seller is responsible for the accuracy, quality, packaging, dispatch and fulfilment of the listed goods. FYNX may provide order-status, communication, delivery-information, buyer-support and dispute tools.

FYNX is not automatically the seller, carrier, warehouse or delivery company for every marketplace order.

## 2. Seller listing and fulfilment duties

Before accepting orders, sellers must provide accurate:
- product identity, condition, description, images and material limitations;
- price and currency, stock/availability and any additional charges;
- dispatch estimate and delivery or pickup options;
- delivery fee, service area and any delivery restrictions;
- return/refund conditions, where legally permitted; and
- seller contact/support details available through FYNX.

Sellers must package items appropriately, dispatch within the stated timeframe, update the order with truthful status/tracking information and promptly notify buyers of delays, inability to fulfil or cancellation. Sellers must not mark an order shipped or delivered without a genuine fulfilment event.

## 3. Delivery and pickup

The fulfilment method and any delivery charge must be shown before the buyer confirms payment. Depending on the order, delivery may be arranged by the seller, a buyer-selected third-party courier/driver, or an agreed pickup. The responsible party, delivery estimate, tracking method and any handover requirements must be disclosed on the order.

Delivery estimates are not guarantees unless explicitly stated. Buyers should provide an accurate delivery address and contact details and make reasonable arrangements to receive the order. Any proof-of-delivery process must be proportionate and must not expose unnecessary personal information.

FYNX must not claim that it operates a fulfilment centre or controls third-party couriers unless that service is actually provided.

## 4. Order inspection and receipt

Buyers should inspect goods promptly after delivery or pickup. If the item is materially different from the listing, damaged, incomplete, counterfeit, unsafe or not received, the buyer should preserve relevant evidence and use the in-app order support/dispute route as soon as possible.

A buyer's receipt confirmation or an order-completion state may affect the platform's protected-order lifecycle. The precise inspection window and effect on seller settlement must be disclosed in the checkout/order UI and confirmed against the production implementation before this policy is finalised.

## 5. Cancellations

Before dispatch, a buyer may request cancellation through the order interface or support route. Cancellation is not complete until the order status confirms it. Sellers must not continue fulfilment after accepting a cancellation unless the buyer agrees or law requires otherwise.

After dispatch, cancellation may be treated as a return request. Made-to-order, perishable, hygiene-sensitive, digital or other legally restricted categories may have different rights only where permitted by applicable law and disclosed before purchase. No term in this policy removes mandatory consumer rights.

## 6. Returns and refunds

A buyer may request a return/refund for non-delivery, damage, missing parts, material misdescription, counterfeit goods, unsafe goods or another reason required by applicable law or the seller's disclosed policy. The request should include the order ID, reason and relevant evidence.

The seller is responsible for responding to a return request and for the product return arrangement unless applicable law, the order terms or an explicitly identified fulfilment provider says otherwise. Return shipping responsibility and costs must be disclosed before purchase where they can be determined. FYNX may facilitate communication and dispute review but does not guarantee a refund before the facts and payment status are verified.

Where a refund is approved, the refund must be issued through the original payment route where supported, subject to provider rules and applicable law. Processing time depends on the payment provider and receiving institution; FYNX must not promise a fixed settlement time without provider confirmation. Payment-processing fees may not be recoverable from the merchant under the provider's terms.

## 7. Disputes and buyer protection

A buyer or seller may raise an order dispute through the available support route. FYNX may request listing details, messages, dispatch/tracking records, delivery evidence, photographs and other relevant information. A disputed order may be restricted from normal completion or settlement while reviewed, where supported by the actual system and provider arrangement.

FYNX's internal order-protection and ledger records are platform accounting/workflow controls; they do not by themselves make FYNX a licensed escrow provider or guarantee that funds are held in a segregated account. Any payment hold, refund or payout is subject to the actual payment provider, approved business configuration, applicable law and disclosed order terms.

## 8. Seller response and support

Seller response times, FYNX support contact, escalation and dispute deadlines must be confirmed and staffed before publication. Do not publish a service-level promise that FYNX cannot meet.
