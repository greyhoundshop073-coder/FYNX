This draft is a working product-policy document, not legal advice or a claim that the required in-app acceptance mechanism is already implemented. Before launch, counsel must review it for the countries in which FYNX operates, and engineering must implement an enforceable versioned acceptance record before a user creates or uploads user-generated content where Google Play requires acceptance.

## 1. The service

FYNX is a social and commerce platform that may include profiles, contact discovery, posts, comments, reactions, reposts, Status/Stories, private and group chat, voice/video calls, camera and media sharing, AI assistance, business profiles, advertising and sponsored placements, a multi-vendor marketplace, order/delivery coordination, reviews, and payment or wallet features when enabled.

Features, currencies, payment methods and monetisation may vary by region and may be unavailable until provider and regulatory approvals are obtained.

## 2. Eligibility and accounts

You must provide accurate account information, protect your credentials, keep contact details current and promptly report suspected account compromise. You may not impersonate another person or business, create accounts to evade enforcement, or use FYNX if you are below the minimum age FYNX formally adopts and publishes. The minimum age and any age-assurance process must be confirmed before publication.

Business and seller accounts must provide accurate ownership, contact, product, pricing, stock, delivery and return information. FYNX or its payment providers may request additional verification. Seller verification requirements and any KYC checks must be accurately described once operational procedures are finalised.

## 3. User content and licence

You retain rights you hold in content you submit. You grant FYNX a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to host, store, reproduce, process, display and transmit that content only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, moderate and improve the services and to provide the features you choose (including feeds, messaging, groups, Status, marketplace listings and advertising placements you authorise). This licence ends when the content is deleted from active services, subject to copies, backups, legal retention and content already shared with other users as explained in the Privacy Policy.

You are responsible for having the rights and permissions needed to upload content, including music, images, videos, trademarks and personal information about others.

## 4. Community standards — prohibited conduct

You must not use FYNX to:
- threaten, harass, bully, stalk, dox, extort, exploit or target people for abuse;
- promote violence, terrorism, exploitation, trafficking, hate or discrimination;
- publish sexual exploitation material or any sexual content involving minors;
- facilitate fraud, phishing, impersonation, scams, money laundering or deceptive fundraising;
- sell or promote illegal, stolen, counterfeit, restricted or dangerous goods or services;
- publish private financial information, passwords, authentication codes, government identifiers or another person's private data without lawful authority;
- distribute malware, attempt unauthorised access, scrape or disrupt the service;
- manipulate reviews, ratings, seller reputation, engagement, orders or payments;
- evade moderation, block controls, account restrictions or lawful requests; or
- use AI tools to create or distribute unlawful, deceptive, abusive or rights-infringing content.

FYNX may maintain additional category-specific restrictions to comply with local law, app-store rules and payment-provider acceptable-use requirements.

## 5. Social, messaging, groups and calls

Users are responsible for the content and recipients they choose. Do not send spam, threats, unwanted sexual content, malicious links or abusive messages. Respect group rules, privacy settings and block decisions. Do not record or redistribute calls or private communications without the required consent and legal authority. Call availability, recording behavior and retention must be described accurately in the final product disclosures.

## 6. Marketplace and seller rules

Sellers must:
- list genuine goods/services they are legally permitted to sell;
- accurately describe condition, authenticity, availability, price, currency, stock and material limitations;
- use images that represent the actual item or clearly disclose illustrative images;
- disclose delivery, pickup, shipping charges, dispatch estimates, returns and refund conditions before purchase;
- fulfil accepted orders or promptly communicate delays, cancellations and stock problems;
- provide truthful tracking and delivery evidence;
- respond reasonably to buyer support and dispute requests; and
- not manipulate orders, reviews, ratings or buyer-protection processes.

Buyers must provide accurate order and delivery information, pay through the displayed supported method and use the in-app dispute/support process honestly. Neither party may submit fabricated evidence or misuse refunds and chargebacks.

FYNX may remove listings, restrict sellers or suspend marketplace access for suspected fraud, unsafe goods, policy violations or legal/provider requirements.

## 7. Advertising and sponsored placements

Where advertising tools are enabled, campaign creators must have rights to their creative material, provide truthful claims, target audiences lawfully and comply with applicable advertising, consumer-protection and payment rules. Paid or sponsored content must be clearly identified. FYNX may reject, pause or remove campaigns that violate law, platform rules or provider requirements. The existence of advertising code or a campaign feature does not mean paid campaigns are active in every environment.

## 8. AI assistance

AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable and must be reviewed before use. Users remain responsible for content they publish or actions they take based on AI output. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials, PINs, private wallet data, government ID numbers or other highly sensitive information to ordinary AI prompts. AI features do not have authority to access private financial records or perform sensitive account actions merely because a user asks.

## 9. Payments, wallet and order protection

Payment, wallet, funding, withdrawal, payout and settlement features are available only when explicitly enabled and approved by the relevant provider and applicable authorities. Test-mode transactions are simulations and are not real funds. Do not describe an internal order ledger or pending state as a regulated bank account or licensed escrow service. Fees, currencies, provider charges and settlement timing must be displayed before a transaction and may vary by approved configuration.

## 10. Delivery, cancellation, returns and disputes

The seller is responsible for accurate fulfilment and the disclosed delivery/pickup arrangement unless the order explicitly identifies another authorised fulfilment provider. Buyers should inspect goods promptly and report non-delivery, damage, material misdescription or suspected fraud through the available support/dispute route. Refund eligibility, deadlines and responsibility depend on the final published Marketplace Shipping, Returns and Refunds Policy and applicable consumer law. FYNX must publish the complete operational policy before enabling live marketplace payments.

## 11. Reports, blocking and moderation

Users should report suspected violations using the in-app reporting controls. Users may block accounts where the feature is available. FYNX may review reported content and take proportionate action, including removing content, restricting features, suspending or terminating accounts, preserving evidence where lawful, and referring matters to appropriate authorities.

A reporting queue alone is not an operational moderation programme. Before launch FYNX must have a monitored moderation contact/rota, escalation process, response targets, trained reviewers and tested report coverage across posts, comments, profiles, chat, groups and marketplace listings.

## 12. Enforcement and appeals

FYNX may act on credible reports, automated risk signals, provider notices, legal requirements or direct observations. Where appropriate, users may appeal an enforcement decision through the published support/appeal route. FYNX will not promise a particular response time until an operational service level is staffed and funded.

## 13. Availability, changes and termination

Features may be changed, suspended or discontinued for maintenance, safety, provider or legal reasons. Users may stop using FYNX and request account deletion through the published process. Account deletion may be subject to verified legal, financial, fraud and dispute-retention obligations described in the Privacy Policy.

## 14. Legal terms to complete

Governing law, dispute forum, liability limitations, warranties, indemnities, consumer rights, notices and contact details require jurisdiction-specific legal review. Do not publish generic clauses that conflict with mandatory consumer protections.
